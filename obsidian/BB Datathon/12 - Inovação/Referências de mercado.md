## Lá fora
- **Lemonade (EUA):** chatbot Maya cota em cerca de 90 segundos com poucas perguntas. Fontes secundárias, números antigos. [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2025/06/18/1-ai-stock-down-75-buy-hand-over-fist-in-june) · [Substack](https://jordanmartenstyn.substack.com/p/lemonade-a-disruptive-insurance-business)

[S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/2025/10/insurers-seek-to-strike-balance-between-benefits-risks-of-ai-and-aerial-imagery) · [Insurify](https://insurify.com/homeowners-insurance/news/insurers-use-airborne-tech-to-inspect-homes/)

[Viu by HUB](https://www.viubyhub.com/the-viu-perspective/2026/08/embedded-insurance-mortgage-closing) · [Fintech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/press-releases/new-research-reveals-70-of-digital-bank-customers-and-44-of-traditional-bank-customers-want-embedded-insurance-offers-based-on-transaction-data)
- **Next best offer em bancos:** um modelo por produto + regra que escolhe a oferta. [Dataiku](https://www.dataiku.com/blog/next-best-offer-banking)
